Groups / families

Create each family or independent group. At settlement time you can set exactly what percentage of the total each group should cover.

People

A person’s percentage is their weight inside their own group. Use 100% for a full adult share, 50% for a half-share child, 25% etc.

1. Split between groups

Set each group’s share of the total holiday spend. The percentages must add to 100%.

2. Split inside each group

Each group’s amount is divided according to member weights. 100 + 100 + 50 means the child carries half an adult share.

Final balances

Positive = should receive money. Negative = owes money.

Safety

Only people who join the room become database members. The app stores names, group membership, share weights and spending entries; it does not need card numbers, bank details or addresses.
