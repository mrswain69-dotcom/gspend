People

Everyone who can be used as a payer appears here. Add people before they install GSpend, record their spending normally, and connect their account later without creating a duplicate.

Families / sharing groups

Use a family/sharing group only when several people should share responsibility together. Edit it to rename the group and add or remove members.

Opening balances

Bring forward money already owed before this spending group started. Opening balances affect settlement but do not count as spending.

Cost split

People with No sharing group are treated as their own responsibility. People inside a family/sharing group are combined and that group’s responsibility is then carried by its adults.

Settlement currencies

Balances are calculated in the spending group base currency. When you mark a payment sent, you can record the currency and amount you actually used.

Spending group settings

Settling creates payment instructions and tracks sent/received confirmations. The group becomes Settled automatically when every required payment is confirmed. Reopening as Active keeps confirmed payment history.

Members & access

Access to the spending group is separate from whether a person counts in the cost split. Owners can remove app access or transfer ownership without deleting historical spending.
